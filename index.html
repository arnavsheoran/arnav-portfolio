Humanoid Robotic Hand / Arm

Designed and developed mechanical components for a humanoid robotic arm using SolidWorks and Autodesk Fusion 360, with attention to fit, assembly and manufacturability.

Developed a tendon-driven finger mechanism using fishing-line tendons and MG995 servo motors. Prepared and optimized parts for FDM 3D printing, then supported servo-control integration using Arduino/ESP32 and Python-based OpenCV vision input.
